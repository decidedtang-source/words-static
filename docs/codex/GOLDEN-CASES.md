## A. SHIFT — ordinary multi-POS word

**Dictionary layer (example, source anchors from current OEWN selection):**
- shift, noun: a **change / transformation**; a **scheduled period of work**.
- shift, verb: **move physically or change direction**; **move to another situation/context**.
- The current wordbook source includes noun source senses shift%1:11:01:: / shift%1:28:00:: and verb shift%2:38:00:: / shift%2:30:03::. Those English source senses are separate from project-written Chinese teaching glosses.
- Common chunks for teaching: night shift, the next shift, shift in priorities, shift focus.

**学习例句**: The supplier needs a work instruction before the next shift.

**我们的简析（default visible）:**
1. **记忆与语义**：记住“变化／转移”和“班次”两组常见意义。当前句子选用“班次”，不强行编一个词源故事将它们推导成同一意思。
2. **句法与概念**：before the next shift 中 shift 是名词，next 修饰它；这句话说明下一班开始前需要作业指导书，但没有规定具体时刻。
3. **辨析**：shift = 班次；handover = 交接工作、信息；changeover = 切换活动。它们可能同时发生，却不相同。
4. **语言游戏与相邻词**：在生产准备讨论中，work instruction、operator readiness、handover 与 next shift 常相联，可能继续核对文件与换班准备。
5. **交际动作与使用**：这句话说明一个有时限的需求，不自动等于正式批准。教学句：Please confirm the instruction before the next shift.

**Reject**: "shift 在这里是转变"、把这句话当作某客户已正式批准生产。

## B. WORKING DEFINITION — compositional phrase and multiple adjective senses

**Dictionary**: working (adj) includes workable/practical, adopted as a temporary basis for further work, functioning (e.g. a machine), serving further work. definition (n) denotes meaning definition; in other contexts also clarity of outline. **working definition** = a definition adopted for current discussion/further work (project's contextual composition, not automatically a standalone OEWN fixed idiom).

**学习例句**: Let us agree on a working definition of a critical defect.

**我们的简析：**
1. **记忆与语义**：“工作性定义；为进一步讨论而采用的定义”。working 可能带暂定/可修改的特点，但绝非所有用法都等于 temporary。
2. **句法与概念**：working 为修饰 definition 的形容词；agree on a definition 是提出取得共识。句子不表明已有最终客户批准。
3. **辨析**：tentative 更偏向不确定，working 更强调可用于当前工作；formal/final 的效力取决于具体制度。
4. **语言游戏与相邻词**：缺陷分类评审常关联 criteria、defect classification、working hypothesis、revision；团队可能继续确认判断口径及证据。
5. **交际动作与使用**：说话者是在建议先形成共用口径，不自动授予批准权限。教学句：Let's agree on a working definition before the review.

**Reject**: global working=temporary, global working=running, or banning any temporary meaning from this context.

## C. OUTCOME FRAMING — derivation and compositional NP

**Dictionary**: outcome (n) result; frame (n) physical/interpretive framework, (v) formulate/present; framing (n) can mean framework or formulation, and the spelling can also be verb frame -ing in other sentences. The **current compound** has outcome as nominal modifier and framing as **noun head**. It is a **contextual composition**; current dictionary is not proven to attest the whole phrase as an idiom.

**学习例句**: Better outcome framing will help management see why the change matters.

**我们的简析：**
1. **记忆与语义**：从结果角度组织、呈现问题。frame 的框架/组织呈现义与 framing 联系，不能把它当成官方英语固定术语。
2. **句法与概念**：outcome 作名词性修饰语，framing 在本词组是名词；句子强调说明变更带来的结果或价值。
3. **辨析**：framing ≠ description ≠ outcome measure：选择理解/呈现角度不同于描述细节或衡量结果的指标。
4. **语言游戏与相邻词**：方案评审可联系 impact、success criteria、decision basis、trade-off；下一步可能比较方案影响。
5. **交际动作与使用**：建议把汇报组织在结果与影响上，并不自动形成正式管理决定。教学句：Frame the proposal around the expected outcome.

**Reject**: mark framing always a verb, mark outcome framing OEWN official idiom, or show only "结果导向表述" without component senses.

## D. TAKE INTO ACCOUNT — idiom, non-literal composition

**Dictionary**: take X into account / take into account X = **consider/include a factor when judging**. Needs attested phrase evidence before assigning a source ID. The current OEWN selected dictionary includes senses of account as an independent word but that **does not authorize fake OEWN whole-idiom definition**.

**本文自编学习例句**: We need to take the measurement uncertainty into account before approving the change.

**我们的简析：**
1. **记忆与语义**：整体记“把某因素考虑进去”，不能把 account 的“银行账户”硬拆进来。
2. **句法与概念**：take X into account 是常见结构；此处把测量不确定性纳入对变更的评价，不是说必须直接否决。
3. **辨析**：consider 比较宽泛；take X into account 更突出不能漏掉某个具体考虑因素。
4. **语言游戏与相邻词**：在变更评审中可能联到 risk、uncertainty、decision basis、approval conditions，进而讨论需要什么证据。
5. **交际动作与使用**：说话者要求考虑证据因素，不等于批准变更。教学句：Please take the supplier's lead time into account.

**Reject**: literal "take bank account", claim current OEWN sample contains whole phrase without evidence.

## E. FUNCTIONAL SEATING SURFACE — technical clarification

**Dictionary + technical evidence**: functional (adj) concerns actual role; seating surface (n phrase) in this mechanical assembly context can refer to a surface through which parts seat/contact. **Which actual surface controls location/load cannot be determined without the drawing, stack and measurement references.** Do not attribute product-specific mechanics to an unverified general dictionary.

**用户学习句**: Please confirm the functional seating surface.

**我们的简析：**
1. **现实问题／记忆**：装配中哪个面实际承靠并影响正确就位？记“功能性承靠面”，不要从英文直接推导具体公差。
2. **句法与概念**：functional 修饰 seating surface；confirm 请求对方确认究竟哪个面发挥该作用，而不是说明已经验证。
3. **辨析**：seating/contact/reference/datum 有关联但不自动等价。涉及轴向/径向定位必须看本产品证据。
4. **语言游戏与相邻词**：可能讨论 contact map、axial location、preload、drawing reference，进一步确认图纸标注及一致测量方法。
5. **交际动作与使用**：请求澄清和确认，不等于正式工程批准。教学句：Please identify the functional seating surface on the drawing.

**Reject**: inventing physical face/datum/tolerance or claiming "confirmed" means customer approved revision.

## F. SCHEDULING CONDITIONAL — sentence, not a fake dictionary entry

**学习句**: If management asks whether the schedule is safe, answer with conditions.

**Key dictionary targets**: whether (introduces indirect question), safe (in schedule context: manageable/credible under assumptions), condition (requirement/contingency). The whole sentence is **not** a dictionary phrase or single word.

**我们的简析：**
1. **记忆与语义**：answer with conditions = 用条件说明判断，不做无条件保证。safe 在这里涉及进度能否稳妥实现，而不是单纯人身安全。
2. **句法与概念**：If ... asks 引导条件从句，whether 引出间接问句，answer 是主句祈使动词；建议按已知条件说明排期判断。
3. **辨析**：safe 与 on track/guaranteed 的侧重点与承诺强度不同；不能因为目前看似可行就变成 guarantee。
4. **语言游戏与相邻词**：项目排程评审可能需要 material readiness、constraints、fallback、decision gate，讨论哪些前提才能维持计划。
5. **交际动作与使用**：属于 **qualify / limit commitment**，提醒管理层汇报采用条件式。教学句：The schedule is achievable provided that materials arrive by Friday.

**Reject**: "safe" only physical safety, or treating conditional answer as unconditional shipment promise.
